Baby Log
Three taps from pocket to logged.
The sheet opens already stamped with the current time and with the next likely entry pre-selected, so the fast path is open → + → Save. Overriding the guess costs exactly one more tap.
01
Tap + — the time is captured the instant the sheet opens.
02
Tap a type — or skip it, the prediction is usually right.
03
Tap Save. Undo waits in the toast.
Screens
Shifts, not just a log
“I need to sleep — feed him at 8 and 1” is a handoff. Katrina taps Hand off, the app drafts the plan from his rhythm, you tap I’ve got him. Feeds you log tick the plan off; when you hand back she gets a shift summary instead of a question.
Platform
Laravel API + installable PWA. Entries write locally first and sync when there’s signal, so 3am logging never waits on a network.
